Customer Segmentation & Churn Analytics

Introduction

Customer churn is one of the major challenges faced by businesses. Retaining existing customers is often more cost-effective than acquiring new ones. This project focuses on analyzing customer data to identify valuable customer segments and predict customers who are likely to leave the company.

The project combines data analysis, customer segmentation, visualization, and machine learning techniques to generate actionable business insights.


Problem Statement

Businesses often struggle to identify customers who are likely to churn. Without proper analysis, organizations may lose valuable customers, resulting in reduced revenue and customer satisfaction.

This project aims to:

* Analyze customer behavior and demographics.
* Identify customer segments based on various attributes.
* Detect patterns associated with customer churn.
* Build a predictive system to support customer retention strategies.


Objectives

* Understand customer demographics and banking behavior.
* Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA).
* Identify key factors influencing churn.
* Segment customers into meaningful groups.
* Visualize important trends and patterns.
* Build a churn prediction model.
* Provide business recommendations for customer retention.

Key Findings

1. Active members show lower churn rates.
2. Older customers are more likely to churn.
3. Customers with fewer products tend to leave more frequently.
4. Geography significantly impacts churn behavior.
5. Credit score influences customer retention.
6. High-balance customers contribute significantly to revenue.
7. Customer engagement reduces churn risk.
8. Multiple product ownership improves retention.
9. Gender-based churn differences are observed.
10. Targeted retention strategies can improve customer loyalty.
